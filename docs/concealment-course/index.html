What chemical concealment is

The drug is not hidden inside the cargo. It is absorbed into the material of the cargo.

Key idea: cocaine hydrochloride dissolves in liquid. Porous materials soaked in it and then dried can look and feel like ordinary cargo. Specialists recover the drug at the destination. The drug itself is not changed into something new.

Why detection fails, and what works

Open all three to continue.

Europol: concealment in carrier materials makes detection at ports extremely difficult. No single tool is enough.

What works

  • Risk profiling of the shipper, consignee, route and goods
  • Document checks: weight, goods description and end user must fit together
  • Chemical monitoring: unusual quantities of common chemicals that do not fit the buyer's business
  • Laboratory analysis of suspect material, sampled and sealed with a clear chain of custody
  • Intelligence sharing with your unit and partner agencies
